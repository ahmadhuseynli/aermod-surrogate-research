# Disclaimer

This repository describes an independent research prototype for atmospheric-dispersion surrogate modelling.

It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency.

AERMOD is used as the reference/teacher model in the research. The surrogate described here is not an approved regulatory substitute for AERMOD and should not be used to replace required regulatory modelling.

The reported results apply only to the restricted experimental domain described in this repository. They should not be extrapolated to other sites, source types, terrain regimes or regulatory applications without new validation.
