# Changelog

## v0.1.2

Editorial refresh of the public research record.

The scientific model and validation results are unchanged. The public narrative was reorganised around the research question, scientific development, practical value, limitations and future direction rather than a date-by-date diary. The validation summary now also gives an intuitive reading of the final normalised-L1 values as roughly 87% whole-field agreement, with the metric boundary stated explicitly.

No model, validation result or scientific conclusion changed from v0.1.0. This update added Zenodo metadata, citation information and permanent DOI references.

## v0.1.0

First public research archive.

The release collected the frozen 2018/2019 temporal-validation results, meteorological-independence checks, research history, known failures, limitations, technical note and GitHub Pages site in one place.

Large teacher data, model weights and the private operational lab were deliberately left out.
