# Research decision and former roadmap

23 October 2026

The earlier roadmap on this project proposed extending the frozen Phase2H prototype through better peak prediction, different climates, variable source physics, terrain and eventually multiple sources. That was the intended route toward a useful independent computation engine. After the later Gen2 and Gen3 experiments, it is no longer an active development plan.

The question is no longer whether to add another modelling feature. The successive tests did not demonstrate an accurate and independently qualified surrogate able to cover enough complete engineering work to justify the product. Gen3's applicability screens sent substantial fractions of candidate hours back to AERMOD. The E04A daily routing test allowed, according to the frozen scope, zero, two or 132 of 1,460 scenario-days to be handled by the surrogate. The terminal V16 challenge passed ten of ten frozen gate families on a blocked 2019 holdout but only two on the spent 2025 transfer population. The later height-column diagnostic explained a material part of that failure but still missed four gates. The final audit also confirmed that the unchanged neural formulation fixed the source configuration instead of accepting the variable stack inputs the original objective required.

Consequently there is no authorized next model, no routine progression to V17, no fresh truth opening and no plan to spend additional computation on repeated changes to the closed formulation. Gen1 Phase2H and mature Gen2 research assets remain frozen. Historical E04 student seals retain their original paused status; their mere existence is not a completed five-year evaluation.

This decision does not forbid atmospheric-dispersion research in general. A future proposal would have to state a genuinely different physical or information premise, show why existing evidence does not already answer it, secure independent confirmation before any truth access, and demonstrate accuracy, applicability and complete-case speed using predeclared tests. It cannot borrow the earlier successes as proof of a new product, or reuse spent validation years as untouched evidence.

For the actual findings and reasons, read [the dated research closure](RESEARCH_CLOSURE_2026-10-23.md). The previous development direction is preserved in commit history, where it belongs as a record of what was proposed before the later tests. The current decision is to stop the generic surrogate product effort.
